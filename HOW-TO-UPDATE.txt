ASHRAE CYPRUS CHAPTER WEBSITE - HOW TO UPDATE
==============================================

PUBLISHING FOR THE FIRST TIME (Netlify)
1. Unzip this package so you have a folder with index.html inside it.
2. Log in at netlify.com, go to Projects / Sites, and drag the whole folder
   onto the "Deploy manually" / drag-and-drop area.
3. Open the Netlify link it gives you and check every page.

CONTACT FORM
After the first deploy, in Netlify open your project > Forms. Under
"Form notifications" add an email notification so messages reach the
chapter inbox. (The form is called "contact".)

ADDING OR CHANGING CONTENT
All content that changes lives in ONE file: site-data.js
- Open it in Notepad or any plain-text editor (not Word).
- It has instructions and examples at the top of each section.
- Events: add one line per event. Past events move to "Past Events"
  automatically by date.
- Board, committee chairs, past presidents, media and sponsors work the same way.
- Photos go in the "photos" folder, sponsor logos in the "sponsors" folder.
- Save the file, then drag the WHOLE folder onto Netlify's Deploys page again.
  The site updates within a minute.

IF SOMETHING BREAKS
Undo your last change in site-data.js (most problems are a missing comma
or quote mark). A broken single entry is skipped, but a missing bracket
can hide a whole section.

CHAPTER EMAIL
Set email: "..." at the top of site-data.js and it appears in the header
bar, footer and contact page. Leave it empty to hide it.
