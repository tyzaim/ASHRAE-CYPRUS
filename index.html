Who we are

What We Do

At ASHRAE Cyprus Chapter we focus on promoting the advancement of technologies for heating, ventilation, air conditioning and refrigeration, through cooperation and community empowerment. We want to be a reference point for all our members and for all operators in the sector, facilitating the sharing of knowledge and resources, and encouraging networking.

We invite you to get in touch with us to find out about all the opportunities. By giving your contribution you can make a difference in achieving our challenging goals.

As a non-profit organisation, we rely on the generosity of our members and supporters to continue our work in advancing the HVAC&R industry through education, research and networking, from technical workshops and seminars to student scholarships and research projects.
